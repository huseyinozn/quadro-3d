A live simulation of Quadro's toolchanges. Motion timing comes from the printer's own Klipper configuration and its parallel-carriage toolchange planner: the parked heads clear the way while the next tool arrives, all in one synchronized move. The machine is the production CAD model; the print is a four-colour 20 mm alignment cube with a prime tower. The console runs the printer's own Klipper configuration, its macros and its toolchange, homing and calibration modules, right in the browser: anything typed there behaves as it would on the machine. Heaters and the bed surface are simulated (the bed follows the printer's saved mesh); load cell forces and accelerometer readings are not.
