A live simulation of Quadro's toolchanges. Motion timing comes from the printer's own Klipper configuration and its parallel-carriage toolchange planner: the parked heads clear the way while the next tool arrives, all in one synchronized move. The machine is the production CAD model; the print is a four-colour 20 mm alignment cube with a prime tower.
